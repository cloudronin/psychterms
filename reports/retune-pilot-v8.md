## Reviewed development proposals for inspection

These are staged development proposals, not published term pages. All have actual two-reader passes and clear copy checks; any field flag remains a publication hold. Source facts and model decisions have not received clinician validation. The controls were authored and labelled by the execution agent, not by a clinician.

### dendrogram

In learning, a dendrogram is a visual display used for hierarchical clustering that shows relationships among variables based on their similarity or calculated distances. In the cited machine-learning context, it was used with heat maps to summarize model statistics and make patterns, important factors, and possible interactions among features easier to see.

Field: learning. Source: [Huang Alexander A; Huang Samuel Y. Dendrogram of transparent feature importance machine learning statistics to classify associations for heart failure: A reanalysis of a retrospective cohort study of the Medical Information Mart for Intensive Care III (MIMIC-III) database](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0288819). CC-BY-4.0.

Source packet SHA-256: `319c0761715e8429db3f12ad6801cea0bbcbc3a0c7ed9199779aea9294f340c4`.

### continuance commitment

In social and organizational research, continuance commitment is one of the three recognized aspects of organizational commitment. In this article, it refers to commitment that arises from the need to continue working.

Field: social. Source: [Anvari Roya; Kumpikaitė-Valiūnienė Vilmantė; Mobarhan Rokhsareh; Janjaria Mariam; Hosseinpour Chermahini Siavash. Strategic human resource management practitioners’ emotional intelligence and affective organizational commitment in higher education institutions in Georgia during post-COVID-19](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0295084). CC-BY-4.0.

Source packet SHA-256: `bc1d801020c71e56ebe52a8e046f856f069d309faa9eaf822694e3310bab4013`.

### family systems theory

In development, family systems theory is presented here as the view that the family system—children, parents, and the family setting, including relationships among family members—strongly shapes child development. In this account, a favorable family environment supports children’s physical and mental development, whereas an unfavorable family environment impedes it.

Field: development. Source: [Sun Li; Li Ao; Chen Meng; Li LuYao; Zhao Yan; Zhu AnQi; Hu Peng. Mediating and moderating effects of authoritative parenting styles on adolescent behavioral problems](https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2024.1336354). CC-BY-4.0.

Source packet SHA-256: `e43bd1d356602fce06118c7ef021cd2d11926d364edb83e0f5c83182412b968f`.

Field: cognition. Source: [Juan Qiao; Shiwan Tao; Yurong Sun; Jiabo Shi; Yu Chen; Shui Tian; Zhijian Yao; Qing Lu. Brain structural and functional abnormalities in affective network are associated with anxious depression](https://doi.org/10.1186/s12888-024-05970-2). CC-BY-4.0.

### situation awareness
