## Source and field preflight amendment

No paid requests were in flight while changing the admission and field rules. Field policy version 3 prioritizes the requested article-supported concept over unrelated words elsewhere in an article. Explicit statistical-method phrases are research methods; a bare “regression” is not assumed statistical. Existing published definitions, fields and verdicts are unchanged. New queue registrations pin the revised helper hashes before dispatch.

The CC BY-only source policy also admits explicitly identified generic CC BY 2.0 and 2.5, alongside 3.0 and 4.0. [CC BY 2.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/) and [CC BY 2.5](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.5/) permit adaptations with attribution. [Creative Commons' adaptation guidance](https://creativecommons.org/faq/#if-i-derive-or-adapt-material-offered-under-a-creative-commons-license-which-cc-licenses-can-i-apply-to-the-resulting-work) explains that the original licence still applies to original material. Every source retains its author, title, edition, original licence link and adaptation notice; PsychTerms' own contributions remain CC BY 4.0. Unversioned notices and NC, ND or SA material stay excluded. Cached XML is rechecked under these rules without another network download.

Further packets prefer a paragraph explicitly explaining the requested term over an incidental abstract mention, capped at 2,400 characters with reproducible character ranges. This changes source selection before registration, not the drafting model or prompt. Every declined source-content hash is retained across alternate-source attempts, so a later decline cannot make an earlier failed packet eligible again. Retrieval skips fallback papers once an entry exists. These changes reduce irrelevant context and repeated work while preserving the original caps.
